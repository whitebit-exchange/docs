Subscribe to the ADL quantile — the 0–4 deleveraging-priority rank for the account's open futures positions — delivered as a push stream instead of a client-side poll of the [ADL quantile endpoint](/api-reference/trading/adl-quantile). A higher quantile means a position closes sooner if a liquidation on that market exhausts order-book liquidity. See [ADL Grade](/concepts/auto-deleveraging#adl-grade) for the ranking mechanism and formula behind the value.

<Note>
This channel does **NOT** support query operations — only subscribe/update/unsubscribe. For a one-time snapshot, call [`POST /api/v4/collateral-account/adl-quantile`](/api-reference/trading/adl-quantile).
</Note>

## Subscribe to ADL quantile

<WsSubscribeSteps
  subscribe={exSubscribeAllPositions}
  subscribeNote="params — empty array subscribes to every futures market where the account has an open position. params[0] (string) subscribes to a single futures market only (e.g. BTC_PERP). Only one ADL quantile subscription is active per connection at a time — a new subscribe call replaces the previous scope rather than adding to it."
  confirmation={exAdlQuantileSubscribeResponse}
  update={exAdlQuantileUpdate}
  updateNote="params — one record per subscribed market, all delivered together in a single ADLQuantile_update message. Each record is a positional array: [market, quantile_long, quantile_short]."
  updateExtra={<WsTupleTable fields={aDLQuantileRecordTupleFields} />}
  unsubscribe={exAdlQuantileUnsubscribe}
/>

<Tip>
The server pushes an update immediately on subscribe, then re-pushes the current quantile for all active subscriptions every **30 seconds**, whether or not a position changed. A client sees a position's updated quantile no later than 30 seconds after the change, without needing to resubscribe.
</Tip>

## Update frequency

The server pushes an update immediately after a successful subscribe, then independently re-pushes the current quantile for all active subscriptions every **30 seconds** — a periodic poll, not an event triggered by a position or mark price change.
