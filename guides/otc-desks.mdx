Over-the-counter (OTC) desks trade large blocks without moving the public order book. WhiteBIT offers OTC block execution alongside the institutional trading, liquidity, and custody surface, arranged through the institutional team rather than a self-serve API funnel.

## What OTC desks can do

- **Trade large blocks** — OTC execution for block transactions with no volume limits and no order-book slippage ([Funds & Institutions](/institutional/overview)).
- **Access deep liquidity** — the institutional trading and liquidity surface, including [portfolio margin](/institutional/overview) and custody, arranged with WhiteBIT.
- **Settle and hold** — institutional custody with the platform's security and compliance posture ([Compliance](/institutional/compliance)).

## How to get started

OTC engagements are handled by the institutional team. Contact `otc@whitebit.com` for block trading, or start from [Partner Solutions](/guides/partner-solutions) to route a broader integration. For onboarding, see [Institutional Onboarding](/institutional/onboarding).

No OTC-specific eligibility, jurisdiction, or settlement conditions are published separately; eligibility is assessed case by case through the institutional onboarding review.
