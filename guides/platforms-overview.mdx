Wallets & White-label puts WhiteBIT behind a partner product as the exchange, custody, and compliance backend. Two offerings cover the range: Wallet-as-a-Service (WaaS) is the wallet and settlement building block, and Crypto-as-a-Service (CaaS) is the full white-label exchange delivered as a dedicated, custom-branded instance. This overview helps a partner choose between the two offerings and links each funnel.

A partner adding wallets or payouts to an existing app starts with WaaS; a partner launching a branded exchange or embedding a full digital-asset product starts with CaaS. CaaS composes the WaaS wallet building block for its end-customer wallets, so the two share the same underlying capabilities.

## What Wallets & White-label provides

- **End-user wallets** — a unique deposit address per end user, with incoming funds reconciled by webhook ([WaaS overview](/guides/waas-overview)).
- **Crypto and fiat payouts** — withdrawals with optional currency conversion, plus fee-free settlement with [WhiteBIT Codes](/platform/whitebit-codes).
- **Institutional custody and compliance** — the security posture of the underlying exchange, with Fireblocks on the custody side and built-in AML controls.
- **A full white-label instance** — a custom-branded exchange integrated through the API, with fiat access available in a second onboarding phase ([CaaS overview](/guides/caas-overview)).

## Who Wallets & White-label is for

- **Banks and EMIs** — embed digital-asset functionality into an existing regulated product.
- **Fintechs and neobanks** — let end customers hold and move digital assets inside the partner app.
- **Wallet and exchange front-ends** — run a branded product on WhiteBIT as the backend.
- **BaaS and core-banking providers** — resell white-label crypto to corporate customers.

## How it works

Wallets & White-label uses WhiteBIT's [integration models](/concepts/integration-models): *managed sub-accounts* (Model A — a sub-account per end user under the partner's master account, with WhiteBIT verifying each user), *attribution* (Model B — each end user holds a personal account connected through a partner key), and a gated *KYC reliance* variant where an eligible partner performs verification. A single pooled account for all users is not offered, because pooled custody conflicts with client-fund segregation and Travel Rule requirements. The final model and any compliance gate are confirmed with WhiteBIT.

## Program terms

Pricing is agreed per contract during onboarding — negotiated per partner rather than a published rate card, and distinct from the Broker program's revenue-share model; the WaaS building block specifically uses bundled pricing with no per-stage charges. ## How to get started

Choose WaaS or CaaS above, then contact `institutional@whitebit.com` with the business case, target segment, and target regions. Complete [institutional onboarding](/institutional/onboarding) — KYB, plus optional fiat access — and clear the [Go-Live Checklist](/best-practices/go-live-checklist) before real volume.
