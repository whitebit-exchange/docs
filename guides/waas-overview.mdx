Wallet-as-a-Service (WaaS) gives each of a partner's end users a sub-account under the partner's
master account, and the partner builds a wallet product on these sub-accounts. This overview covers fit
and the verification variants. For the technical integration, see the
[Developer guide](/guides/waas-integration).

<Tip>
The [WaaS Recipes](/guides/waas-recipes) show runnable examples of deposit addresses,
reconciliation, payouts, and settlement.
</Tip>

## What WaaS provides

- A [sub-account](/products/sub-accounts/overview) per end user, with independent balances and API keys.
- A crypto deposit address for each sub-account, once the account manager enables crypto deposits to
  sub-accounts for the account.
- Crypto withdrawals from each sub-account, each approved by the master account through
  [sub-account withdrawal approval](/products/sub-accounts/quickstart#sub-account-withdrawal-approval).
- Conversion with [Convert](/platform/convert) on each sub-account.
- Spot trading, including market orders, on every sub-account with no enablement request to the account
  manager; the partner controls trading through the sub-account's spot permission (see
  [Account architecture](/guides/sub-account-integration#account-architecture)).
- Fee-free transfers between the master account and each sub-account.
- Fiat payouts from the master account.

## Choose a verification variant

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Managed sub-accounts" icon="sitemap">
    Each end user gets a sub-account under the partner's master account. **WhiteBIT verifies (KYC)
    each user**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sub-accounts with KYC reliance" icon="handshake" href="/guides/waas-integration#kyc-reliance-eligibility">
    Managed sub-accounts, but **WhiteBIT relies on the partner's KYC** of the end user. Requires a
    compliance assessment and agreement — see the eligibility conditions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shared-KYC sub-accounts" icon="id-card" href="/guides/waas-integration#build-path-per-variant">
    Each sub-account **shares the master account's verification**. WhiteBIT verifies the partner
    (KYB); the partner verifies its own end users.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The three variants share one sub-account architecture and differ in who verifies the end users. With
managed sub-accounts, WhiteBIT verifies each end user. With KYC reliance, the partner verifies, and
WhiteBIT relies on that verification under an agreement. With shared-KYC sub-accounts, each sub-account
carries the master account's verification: WhiteBIT verifies the partner during institutional onboarding
(KYB), and the partner verifies its own end users. The final choice and any compliance gate are confirmed
with WhiteBIT. For end users with personal WhiteBIT accounts, the partner
integrates through [App Builders](/guides/app-builders-overview) instead. A partner that keeps each end
user's balance in the partner's own ledger, with one WhiteBIT account and a deposit address per end user,
uses [Crypto-as-a-Service](/guides/caas-overview); see
[Partner Solutions](/guides/partner-solutions#choose-an-integration-path).
[Integration models](/concepts/integration-models#comparison) compares sub-accounts with the partner's own account
and with customers' own accounts.

## Program terms

Pricing is agreed per contract during onboarding — negotiated per partner rather than a published
rate card, and distinct from Embedded Trading's revenue-share model. WaaS uses bundled pricing
with no per-stage charges. Eligibility for the gated KYC-reliance variant is assessed case by case
per the conditions above.

## Compliance at a glance

- **Travel Rule (EEA and Turkey):** transfers must carry sender/recipient data. Inbound deposits
  for EEA/Turkey accounts are held until verification completes; withdrawals carry a travel-rule
  payload. See [Travel Rule](/concepts/travel-rule).
- **MiCA (EEA):** some stablecoin operations are restricted for EEA users, which affects the
  assets available to those users. See [Regulatory Compliance](/institutional/compliance).

## How to apply

1. Complete KYB as part of [Institutional Onboarding](/institutional/onboarding). Fiat access is a
   separate second phase, not automatic after KYB.
2. Create API keys and configure webhooks in the WhiteBIT dashboard.
3. Request the enablements the product needs through the account manager — sub-accounts, crypto
   deposits to sub-accounts, and the sub-account withdrawal endpoints. See
   [Sub-Accounts](/products/sub-accounts/overview).
4. Work through the [Go-Live Checklist](/best-practices/go-live-checklist) before real volume.

To get started or discuss a verification variant, contact institutional@whitebit.com or the account manager.
