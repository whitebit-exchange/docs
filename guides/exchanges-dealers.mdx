Exchanges, trading venues, and dealers connect to WhiteBIT for liquidity, market data, and venue connectivity. These are partnership engagements arranged with the institutional team, with public market data available directly.

## What exchanges and dealers can do

- **Access liquidity** — route flow to WhiteBIT's order books through the Liquidity Providing program ([Funds & Institutions](/institutional/overview)).
- **Consume market data** — public prices, order-book depth, and trades over REST and WebSocket, no API key required ([Market Data](/products/market-data/overview)).
- **List WhiteBIT as a venue** — connect an order or execution management system that references WhiteBIT as a trading venue, arranged with the institutional team ([Funds & Institutions](/institutional/overview)).

## How to get started

Venue, liquidity, and connectivity engagements are handled by the institutional team — contact `institutional@whitebit.com`, or start from [Partner Solutions](/guides/partner-solutions) to route the integration. Public market data needs no agreement; see the [Market Data overview](/products/market-data/overview).
