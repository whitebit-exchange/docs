sequenceDiagram

    participant W as WhiteBIT

    Note over P,W: Wait until pending_deposit_amount is 0<br/>(main-account/balance, sub-account key)

            P->>W: POST /api/v4/main-account/history (sub-account key, withdrawals)
        end

        P->>W: POST /api/v4/sub-account/transfer (main-account key, sub-account to main account)

    end
    loop Until the balances show nothing left to move
        P->>W: POST /api/v4/sub-account/balances (main-account key, sub-account ID)
        W-->>P: Remaining amount on each balance
    end
    opt After a sale
        P->>F: Credit the end user's fiat
    end
    P->>W: POST /api/v4/sub-account/block (main-account key, sub-account ID)
    Note over P,W: From here on, the sub-account<br/>key is refused with 401
